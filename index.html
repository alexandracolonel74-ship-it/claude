Living Room Redesign Proposal

Coastal Organic — Refined Warmth Concept

Overview

This proposal builds on the room's existing strengths — its generous proportions, exposed dark-timber ceiling beams, and open connection to the dining area — and evolves the space toward a warmer, more intentional "coastal organic" aesthetic.

The goal is to add tactile richness and curated character while preserving the airy, light-filled quality that defines the room today.

Coastal Organic Neutral Palette Biophilic Open Plan

What's Working

The exposed timber beams are a stand-out feature and anchor the entire scheme.

The limestone-effect floor tiles unify the living and dining zones beautifully.

The open-plan flow to the kitchen and dining area creates a sociable, relaxed atmosphere.

The existing black-and-white portrait gallery adds cultural depth and personality.

Opportunities

Seating arrangement can be tightened for better conversation flow.

Layering more texture — linen, aged leather, ceramic — will add warmth.

Lighting needs a cohesive strategy; the existing floor lamp feels isolated.

Colour Palette

Key Design Changes

🛋

Seating Reconfiguration

Rotate the main sofa 15° to face the fireplace, creating a true hearth focal point. Add a pair of low linen armchairs opposite to encourage conversation rather than a TV-forward layout.

💡

Layered Lighting

Introduce three pendant lights above the coffee table area using woven rattan shades echoing the existing floor pendant. Add dimmable wall sconces flanking the gallery wall.

🪴

Biophilic Layering

Expand the plant collection with a large fiddle-leaf fig in the corner near the French doors. Add a trailing pothos shelf above the gallery. Retain and celebrate the existing Ficus.

🧶

Textile Refresh

Swap current throw pillows for a mix of Belgian linen, bouclé, and hand-woven cotton in the palette above. Add a second, smaller kilim rug to define the reading corner.

🪵

Coffee Table Styling

Keep the existing reclaimed-wood table — it's a hero piece. Style it with a cluster of varied-height ceramic vessels, a stack of art books, and a single trailing succulent.

🖼

Gallery Wall Extension

Extend the existing 4-portrait row into a floor-to-ceiling asymmetric salon hang, mixing the black-and-white portraits with a few smaller abstract pieces in complementary warm tones.
